## Methodology corner

The preseason snap-chart discipline and the injury red-alert protocol were both new enough to write down; they're appended to `data/intel/expert_methods.md`. The two that will change how I work:

**Who rests is as informative as who plays.** A locked-in starter sitting with no reported injury is the team telling you the job is settled — Tre Tucker, Barion Brown and David Montgomery all rested this weekend. The inverse holds: a high-pedigree rookie playing with the backups (Stowers) is a negative signal, not a neutral one. **Exit order** is the finer version — Malik Davis started *and left before* Jaydon Blue, the reverse of the prior week, which is what reopened the Dallas RB2 question.

**Injury red-alert protocol.** When a scare breaks, move the player down a little and raise his risk rating — don't swing to season-ending without a diagnosis. Sites that pulled Jeanty from rankings entirely created a market where his backup went in round 4 on zero information, and dynasty managers paid a first, a 2027 second, Blake Corum and Brian Thomas Jr. inside 24 hours. Also: **discount every injury vacancy for the post-cutdown veteran signing.** Sal used that rule to predict Arizona would waive Benson before Conner, and the wire confirmed it the same day.
