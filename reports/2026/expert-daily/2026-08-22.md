## Methodology corner

Five new lessons added to `expert_methods.md`, the two most useful:

- **FF's early-down heuristic**: ~90% of RB opportunities come on early downs, so a missing receiving profile is an overrated knock on late-round RBs with early-down + goal-line roles (their Mason case).
- **Sal's anchor-RB fragility principle**: once you anchor on elite RBs, skip mid-round insurance RBs entirely ("if Gibbs gets hurt I'm not winning anyway") — spend those picks on a WR portfolio where one of three bench WRs hitting covers a fragile starter. Directly applicable to the MEXICA Hero-RB build.

Also noted: in *sharp* rooms QB/TE fall and RB/WR get pushed up — the opposite of Sleeper home leagues, so our platform-list rule stands for your drafts.
